# RAMS licence boundary

The official JHU RAMS release separates annotation rights from source-text rights. Annotations are
offered under CC-BY-SA-4.0; JHU states it does not own the underlying news text. Research use may
proceed locally. Raw text and text-bearing public artifacts are blocked from redistribution by
default. The current acquisition target is RAMS 1.0c; its archive and extracted files must be hashed.
